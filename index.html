Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the BitMine platform.

What is BitMine: BTC Cloud Mining?

BitMine: BTC Cloud Mining is a cloud mining management and simulation platform that lets users coordinate remote mining nodes. The app acts as a dashboard to claim earnings, boost mining speed, and request payouts securely.

Will mining damage or heat up my mobile device?

No. Traditional mining runs heavy calculations on hardware, leading to battery degradation and overheating. BitMine runs all computations on secure remote servers. Your phone is only used as a tracking dashboard.

What is the minimum withdrawal limit?

The standard minimum withdrawal threshold is 0.00001 BTC. Payout requests are verified and processed directly to TRC20/BTC wallet addresses within 24 to 72 hours.

How do hashrate boosts work?

You can temporarily boost your hashrate power by claiming daily bonuses, activating the sponsor video multipliers, or by referring active users to the platform.
